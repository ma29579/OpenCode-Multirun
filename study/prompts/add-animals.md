The farm now keeps chickens, ducks and geese. Add a new migration `V8__create_poultry_tables.sql` in `db/migrations/` that creates the tables for them.

For every bird the farm records its name, its birth date, its weight, the stable it lives in and whether it is a laying bird. For the laying birds the farm also records how many eggs each bird laid on each day and the average weight of these eggs.

Follow the database guidelines that apply to this project. Do not change existing migrations.
